## 1262978 Cookie's Feast

Cookie is the murloc who cooks for the Defias in the Deadmines, and whoever took this recipe from him did not ask nicely. Two portions of mystery meat, one of heavy kodo and soothing spice, slow roasted together until the kodo softens and the mystery deepens. I can smell what the mystery meat was. In Cookie's kitchen, I would rather not have known. A feast is for sharing, so lay it on one great platter and let everyone reach. My deathguards do this in silence, carefully, each one leaving a little for the next, which is more than the Defias ever did. The smell fills the hall and draws the living down from the city above, and I have stopped turning them away. A murloc criminal's feast, in a dead woman's kitchen, eaten by whoever is hungry. I think Cookie would approve. I think he would want his ladle back.

## 1225763 Grand Lobster Banquet

A banquet is a great deal of work for one evening, and this one is more than most: twelve darkclaw lobsters, four portions of turtle, three buzzard wings and exquisite spices, which are exquisite mostly in price. The turtle stews longest, so it begins first. The buzzard roasts while the turtle stews. The lobsters go last, poached in batches and lifted the moment their shells turn red, before the smell sharpens to copper. Everything must reach the table together, and you will know it is ready when the hall smells of the sea, the forest and the hearth all at once. I have cooked this twice, for the Dark Lady's officers, who said nothing and ate everything. The second time, one of them came down to the kitchen afterwards to tell me it was very good. He had not eaten a lobster since he was alive. He remembered what one was supposed to be.

## 1225762 Specklefin Feast

Twelve spotted yellowtail, three rockscale cod, four portions of raptor and exquisite spices, laid out on the largest board in the kitchen. The cod go first, because they are thick. The raptor roasts and rests while the fish cook. The yellowtail go last, because they are quick, and the moment their skin lifts from the grill they are done. The smell of a fish feast is a tide coming in: first the sea, then the smoke, then the spice. When the spice arrives, start carrying. I made it for the zeppelin crews at the end of a long season, and the goblins, who are not sentimental, sat down at my counter and ate for an hour without once mentioning money. That is the only time I have seen a goblin forget to haggle. It was the feast, not me.
