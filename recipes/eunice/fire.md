## 1229737 Basic Campfire

Before the food, the fire. Simple wood, stacked loosely so the air can move through it, lit at the bottom and left alone. You will hear when it is ready: the crackle settles into a low hum, and the flames sink into coals. Cook on the coals, never the flames. I teach this first, before the wolf, because a cook without a fire is a guest, and I do not keep guests in my kitchen.

## 1283400 Journeyman Campfire

Star wood burns hotter and cleaner than common wood, with a smell like resin and frost. The recipe came out of the Wailing Caverns, where the druids dreamed wrong, and someone decided a better campfire was worth the trip. Stack it the same way and let it burn down further before you cook, because a hotter fire forgives less. When the coals glow white at the edges, you are ready.

## 1291341 Expert Campfire

Thick logs, slow to catch and slow to die, a fire for the whole of an evening. Lay them in a square with a gap for the air and light them from beneath. Then wait. The smell changes when it is ready, from green smoke to dry, from sharp to sweet. That is an expert's fire, and an expert's lesson: the best heat is the one you waited for. I have not been sold this recipe. I build it from what I was told, and I was told well.

## 1263067 Iron Oven

Flour, spring water and soothing spice: that is the whole recipe for an iron oven. I believe the dough is worked into the new oven and baked hard, to season it, as a new pan is seasoned. I have never seen it for sale. A goblin trader told me of it, thinking I would be impressed. I was. I did not tell him. Goblins are unbearable once they know they have impressed you, and they put it on the bill.
