## 1262978 Cookie's Feast

Cookie is the murloc who keeps the kitchen of the Defias Brotherhood, deep in the Deadmines. I am told he cooks with a ladle in one hand and a cleaver in the other, and does not much mind which he uses on visitors. His recipe was carried out of the mine by adventurers who took it from him, and I have studied it with a professional interest I am not proud of. Two portions of mystery meat and one of heavy kodo, slow roasted with soothing spice until the kodo yields and the mystery meat has gone dark. It is a feast, which means it is made to be shared from a common platter, which I find unhygienic and the Defias, presumably, find necessary. The dish is excellent. The cook is a murloc and a criminal. I would hire him tomorrow, if he would leave the cleaver at the door.

## 1225763 Grand Lobster Banquet

A banquet is not a dish. It is a campaign. Twelve darkclaw lobsters, four portions of turtle, three buzzard wings and a pouch of exquisite spices, which are so called because the merchant who sells them needs a word for the price. The lobsters are poached, the turtle stewed, the buzzard roasted, and all of it brought to the table at once, which is the true difficulty. Every part must finish at the same moment, so begin with the slowest, the turtle, and end with the fastest, the lobsters, lifted out the instant they turn red. I have cooked a banquet like this for the nobility of Stormwind, and I will tell you what I learned: the guests remember the lobster, and the cook remembers the timing. Nobody remembers the buzzard. Cook it well anyway. Somebody always notices what you skipped.

## 1225762 Specklefin Feast

Specklefin feast is the banquet of a cook who had a great deal of fish and one raptor's worth of ambition. Twelve spotted yellowtail, three rockscale cod and four portions of raptor, with exquisite spices, which earn their name at the counter if not always in the pan. The fish are grilled whole and the raptor roasted, and the trick, as with every feast, is that it all arrives together. The cod, being thick, start the work. The yellowtail, being quick, finish it. The raptor rests while the fish cook, which suits everyone. Lay it out on the largest board you own, the raptor in the middle like a general and the fish around it in ranks. A feast is a performance. The audience eats it. You, the cook, bow afterwards and eat whatever is left, which is usually the cod.
